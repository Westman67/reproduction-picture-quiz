# Reproduction quiz Step 1 relevance review

This pass screened the stems and keys of all 705 questions in the previous release. It emphasized clinically transferable anatomy and histology, mechanisms, useful differential findings, stain interpretation, and treatment reasoning supported by the supplied lectures. It removed items that primarily tested incidental shapes, colors or positions; instrument routing; exact procedural steps; or a substantially repeated learning objective. Foundational visual recognition remains when the pattern helps distinguish a structure or disease. The result is 617 scored questions: 27 Physiology, 437 Pathology, and 153 questions in 33 Clinical Cases.

All 212 feedback flags from the prior [visual reasoning audit](visual-reasoning-20261112.md) received a disposition. The review rewrote 147 retained questions' distractor feedback and moved the other 65 flagged items to the reference archive. There are 88 archived questions in all, including 23 other items found in the relevance screen. This was a relevance and feedback pass, not independent clinical revalidation of every unflagged option or of the 19 existing third party questions. No new scored questions were created and no keys were moved.

The transobturator sling route and other device-placement details are no longer scored. R23 retains the stress leakage and distended bladder examples, with feedback that explains the mechanism or clinical inference (R23 Clinical GU II RLS, pp. 27 and 35; R23 Clinical GU II transcript, pp. 7 and 9). R21 now connects the plotted rise in plasma and red cell volumes to hemodilution (R21 Clinical Obstetrics Hypertension in Pregnancy RLS, p. 10; transcript, p. 2). Three quiz variants conceal answer labels while preserving the tissue and arrows (R9 Reproductive Histology RLS, p. 30; R23 Clinical GU II RLS, p. 27; R25 Sex Hormones Pharmacology III RLS, p. 41).

The [per-question ledger](step1-review-20261113.csv) records the disposition and citations for every reviewed item. The [reference archive](step1-retired-20261113.json) preserves the original records, including the R27 case that tested sentinel mapping and an exact adjuvant regimen. Teaching originals remain intact. Saved attempts, marks, and flag notes remain in browser history. A resumed session skips retired items; an entirely archived case keeps its attempt and exam history but no longer appears in the scored case menu.
