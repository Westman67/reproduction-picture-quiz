# Third Step 1 relevance screen

This pass screened 546 scored questions and reassessed all 159 archived questions from the [first](step1-retired-20261113.json) and [second](step1-second-screen-archive.json) passes. It archived 25 scored drills, including 17 repeated tasks, and restored two archived drills with revised explanations and the same keyed positions. The quiz now has 523 scored questions across 22 physiology drills, 348 pathology drills, and 153 questions in 33 clinical cases.

- R17 page 11 now asks learners to distinguish the opaque fetal membranes in the chorioamnionitis example from the clearer normal membranes. Feedback explains the limits of the gross finding.
- R19 pages 94–95 now use the normal hysteroscopic field as a control for intrauterine adhesions in Asherman syndrome. The restored question links the absence of bridging bands to the comparison.
- The newly archived drills include questions that repeat fetal station, molar pathology and the R27 ovarian imaging cases. The archive also includes a diagram whose printed labels give away the answer and a cervical drawing whose key is a panel number.

The [decision ledger](step1-third-screen.csv) lists the status and citation of all 705 questions reviewed. The [new archive](step1-third-screen-archive.json) preserves the original records for the drills moved out of scoring. The earlier archive files are historical snapshots. This pass applied a relevance screen and checked lecture support for the restored items. It was not an independent clinical validation of the full bank.
