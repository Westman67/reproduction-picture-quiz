# Reproduction visual reasoning audit and expansion

The quiz now has 705 scored items. This release adds 42 visual reasoning questions to the 663-item baseline. There are 27 physiology drills, 514 pathology drills and 164 questions across 34 clinical cases. Seven cases are new and eleven existing cases gained a follow-up. All 42 additions require an inference from the displayed image or table. There are 24 second order and 18 third order additions. Ten questions test stain selection or interpretation, five test treatment, eleven test interpretation or a next clinical step, and sixteen test mechanism or related inference.

This is a targeted content pass following the [full structural and asset audit](question-audit-20261112.md). It replaces repeated distractor explanations in 117 pathology questions without moving the original keyed options. The other 212 previously flagged drills still need individual distractor review. An unresolved feedback flag does not on its own mean the key is wrong. The prior inventory covered 143 PDFs and one Word file. This release verifies the sources of the added material and does not assert a full clinical reread of all 4,830 PDF pages. The 19 existing third party items remain in the Source type filter. Their external citations were not medically revalidated in this course-only review.

Treatment questions follow the specific case and cited slide. The R27 postoperative table reproduces the decisive staging facts from page 46. The R28 chest radiograph uses the complete embedded panel on page 24. The R30 inference uses the single RLS version and compares the hormone patterns; it avoids the ambiguous axis abbreviation in the slide.
